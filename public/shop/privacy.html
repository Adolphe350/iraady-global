Privacy policy

Iraady: Business Software (mobile app) · Iraady Ltd · Last updated 7 November 2026

In short: the Iraady app has no accounts and collects no personal data. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

What the app does with data

When you open it, the app downloads the public product list from iraady.com. Your phone keeps a saved copy so you can browse offline; you can remove it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Requesting a demo

Choosing "Request a demo" opens your own email or WhatsApp app with a message ready to send. The message reaches Iraady Ltd only if you send it, and we use it only to reply to you.

Links

Links to products open their own websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
