Privacy policy

FormsPro: Offline Surveys · Iraady Ltd · Last updated 6 November 2026

In short: FormsPro collects only what it needs to work for field teams of organisations that use FormsPro. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Your name and email address, used to sign in.
  • The answers you collect in forms, which can include text, photos and GPS location when a form asks for them.
  • A device identifier used to keep offline records and sync them safely.

How we use it

Only to provide the app: to sign you in, show and sync your information, and send notifications you have allowed. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

Who is responsible

Your organisation owns the forms and the data collected with them and is responsible for it. Iraady hosts the service for your organisation.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. We use hosting providers (Amazon Web Services) to store data and send email, under contracts that require them to protect it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access is limited to people who need it to run the service.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When your account is deleted, your personal data is removed within 30 days, except records the law requires us or your organisation to keep.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data. See how to delete your account.

Children

FormsPro is not directed at children under 13.
