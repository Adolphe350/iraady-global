How to play

  • Pick a build from the workshop. Every build starts as a few big chunks.
  • Pull a part up out of the parts bin and drop it on its dashed outline.
  • Each stage splits the same object into more pieces — down to every screw and cable.
  • Stages with many pieces hide the labels: identify each part by its shape.
  • Stuck? Each stage gives you three hints that reveal where a part belongs.
  • Finish a stage without mistakes for three stars. Clear every stage to master a build.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up and no login.

Does it need internet? No. The game works completely offline.

How do I reset my progress? Delete and reinstall the app. Progress is stored only on your device.

Is there any cost or advertising? No. The app is free and contains no ads and no in-app purchases.
