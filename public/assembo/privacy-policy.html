Privacy Policy

Assembly Workshop (Assembo) · Last updated 15 August 2026

In short: Assembly Workshop collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no server. The game runs entirely on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect, store or transmit any personal information. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or usage analytics.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress — which stages you have completed, stars earned and best times — in its own private storage on your device. This information never leaves your device, is not readable by us or anyone else, and is permanently removed when you delete the app.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics or crash-reporting services, no social media integrations and no third-party tracking of any kind. Because no data is collected, no data is shared or sold.

Network use

Assembly Workshop works fully offline. It does not require an internet connection and does not send data to any server.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. It contains no user-generated content, chat, social features or in-app purchases, and it does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date above.
