Assembo

Speedfy

Does it track me in the background? Only if you turn on Always-On Tracking in Settings. You can turn it off at any time.

Where are my trips stored? On your device. When you are signed in and online, trips (including GPS points) are also uploaded to our servers. "Clear All History" removes data from this device only; deleting your account removes it from our servers.
