Delete your LoanDesk account

LoanDesk: Loan Manager (Iraady Loans) · Iraady Ltd

How to ask for deletion

  1. Email info@iraady.com from the email address you use to sign in.
  2. Use the subject “Delete my LoanDesk account”.
  3. We confirm and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account and sign-in details, your profile (name, email, phone) and the data linked to your account.

What may be kept

Records your organisation or lender must keep by law (for example financial or programme records) stay with them for the period the law requires. Backups are overwritten within 30 days.
