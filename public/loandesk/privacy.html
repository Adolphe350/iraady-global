Privacy policy

LoanDesk: Loan Manager (Iraady Loans) · Iraady Ltd · Last updated 6 November 2026

In short: LoanDesk collects only what it needs to work for customers and loan officers of lenders that use Iraady Loans. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and phone number, which your lender uses to set up your account.
  • Loan information: loans, payment schedules, payments and receipts recorded by your lender.
  • A device token used only to send you payment reminders (push notifications), if you allow notifications.

How we use it

Only to provide the app: to sign you in, show and sync your information, and send notifications you have allowed. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

Who is responsible

Your lender decides what loan data is recorded and is responsible for it. Iraady hosts the service for your lender.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. We use hosting providers (Amazon Web Services) to store data and send email, under contracts that require them to protect it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access is limited to people who need it to run the service.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When your account is deleted, your personal data is removed within 30 days, except records the law requires us or your organisation to keep.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data. See how to delete your account.

Children

LoanDesk is not directed at children under 13.
